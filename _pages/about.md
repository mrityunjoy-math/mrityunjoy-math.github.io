I am an **Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering** at **[Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) University](https://www.soa.ac.in)**, Bhubaneswar, Odisha, where I have been working since October 2023.

I completed my **PhD in Mathematics at IIT Guwahati** under the supervision of **[Prof. Srinivasan Natesan](http://iitg.ac.in/natesan)**. My doctoral research focused on parameter-uniform numerical methods for singularly perturbed parabolic convection–diffusion–reaction problems, including alternating direction implicit and hybrid schemes for two-dimensional problems with small parameters.

Before my PhD, I earned my **MSc in Mathematics and Computing** at **[IIT Guwahati](https://www.iitg.ac.in)** and my **BSc in Mathematics (Honours)** at the **[University of North Bengal](https://www.nbu.ac.in/)**, West Bengal.

My research lies in the **uniform analysis of efficient numerical methods for singular perturbation problems**. I am interested in designing and analysing robust numerical methods, such as layer-adapted meshes and splitting schemes, that remain accurate uniformly with respect to small perturbation parameters.
