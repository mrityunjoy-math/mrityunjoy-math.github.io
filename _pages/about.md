I am an **Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) University**, Bhubaneswar, Odisha, where I have been working since October 2023.

I completed my **PhD in Mathematics at IIT Guwahati** under the supervision of **[Prof. Srinivasan Natesan](http://iitg.ac.in/natesan)**. My doctoral research focused on parameter-uniform numerical methods for singularly perturbed parabolic convection–diffusion–reaction problems, including alternating direction implicit and hybrid schemes for two-dimensional problems with small parameters.

Before my PhD, I earned my **MSc in Mathematics at IIT Guwahati** and my **BSc at the University of North Bengal**, West Bengal.

My research lies in the **numerical analysis of singular perturbation problems**. I am interested in designing and analysing robust numerical methods, such as layer-adapted meshes and splitting schemes, that remain accurate uniformly with respect to small perturbation parameters.
